# Generated Image Direction

These are the target image prompts for the next asset pass.

## 1. Onboarding Hero

- Use case: `photorealistic-natural`
- Asset type: app onboarding hero
- Primary request: candid small-group meetup in a warm cafe, 4 to 6 adults in their late 20s to early 40s, natural light, intimate but calm, no exaggerated laughter, no stock-photo energy
- Composition: editorial crop with negative space, table edge visible, layered depth, one or two faces partially turned away
- Mood: relaxed, premium, believable, warm but not orange
- Avoid: staged corporate smiles, laptops, obvious dating-app energy, over-styled fashion shoot look

## 2. Home Reserved Meetup Atmosphere

- Use case: `photorealistic-natural`
- Asset type: reserved meetup hero image
- Primary request: evening dinner meetup atmosphere, soft restaurant lighting, modern European interior, place setting and human presence without making it feel like a restaurant ad
- Composition: cinematic horizontal crop with depth, enough dark space for overlay UI
- Mood: anticipation, calm social energy
- Avoid: empty restaurant, luxury cliché, wine-commercial styling

## 3. Meetup Card Series

- Use case: `photorealistic-natural`
- Asset type: meetup card media
- Primary request: short series of candid atmospheres for coffee walk, dinner, drinks, board games, museum evening
- Composition: tighter crops, tactile details, place-first framing
- Mood: intimate, grown-up, soft, credible
- Avoid: generic tourist photography, staged posing, youth-culture party look

## 4. Circle Screen

- Use case: `photorealistic-natural`
- Asset type: post-meetup connection artwork
- Primary request: close, human, low-pressure group moment after meeting in person, subtle portrait grouping, warm interior or twilight exterior
- Composition: layered faces and shoulders, shallow depth of field, emotionally quiet
- Mood: closeness without sentimentality
- Avoid: friend-group cliché, big gestures, obvious social-media pose

## 5. Profile Portrait Style

- Use case: `photorealistic-natural`
- Asset type: profile portrait reference direction
- Primary request: clean natural portrait, soft daylight, honest expression, crisp but not over-retouched, adult and trustworthy
- Composition: head-and-shoulders, eye level, subtle background blur
- Mood: open, calm, real
- Avoid: glamour lighting, beauty retouching, hard studio flash
